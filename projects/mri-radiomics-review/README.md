# MRI Radiomics and the Tumor Microenvironment

**Evidence level:** group literature review and presentation; not an original clinical experiment

## Project overview

This academic presentation synthesized literature on how multiparametric MRI and radiomics may characterize tumor cellularity, vascularity, oxygenation, metabolism, and spatial heterogeneity.

## Problem

Biopsy provides localized and invasive information, while imaging can sample the whole tumor non-invasively. The translational challenge is determining whether quantitative imaging features are reproducible, biologically meaningful, interpretable, and useful in clinical decisions.

## Research question

How can MRI-derived radiomic features be linked to the tumor microenvironment, and what methodological barriers prevent those features from becoming reliable clinical biomarkers?

## Results

No original quantitative result is claimed. The review identified recurring themes in the literature:

- acquisition and scanner variability limit reproducibility;
- manual or semi-automatic segmentation introduces operator variation;
- small single-center cohorts limit generalization;
- radiomic features need biological and external validation;
- black-box models can limit trust and actionable use; and
- standardized reporting and prospective validation are needed for translation.

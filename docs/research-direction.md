# Research Interests: Healthcare AI as a Human System

## Motivation

My current technical work exposes recurring human-centered problems: unsupported generated content, incorrect citations, unreliable validation under distribution shift, interpretability limits, privacy constraints, and the difficulty of integrating prototypes into real work.

## Questions of interest

I am interested in how human factors and ergonomics can help evaluate healthcare AI beyond model accuracy. Questions I am learning about include:

- task accuracy and time;
- cognitive workload and usability;
- appropriate trust and reliance;
- understanding of evidence and uncertainty;
- error detection and recovery;
- communication across patients and clinical teams; and
- workflow, responsibility, and safety after implementation.

Methods I am learning about include usability studies, workload and usability measures, interviews, think-aloud protocols, interaction logs, and workflow mapping. This is a list of interests, not a claim that these studies have been completed.

## Connection to this portfolio

- The patient-education prototype motivates research on comprehension, source checking, and human oversight of generative AI.
- The biomedical classification experiment motivates research on how validation scope and uncertainty should be communicated to users.
- The radiomics review motivates research on interpretability, annotation variability, and clinical translation.
- Master's research is ongoing. Only its broad area—AI applications in MRI—is shared here.
